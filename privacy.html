Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

Edge Journal is run by Slino Studios ("we"). This policy explains what we collect when you use Edge Journal, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address. If you sign in with Google (or another provider), we also receive your name and profile photo from that provider. We never see your Google password.
  • Your journal. The trades, prices, P&L, notes, emotions, mistakes, daily plans and reviews you enter, plus your account rules and settings.
  • Basic technical data. Our hosting and database providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address and browser type) to run and secure the service.

We do not use advertising trackers or sell your data.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and show you your own journal on any device.
  • To calculate your statistics, reports and insights. These are computed in your browser from your own data.
  • To send account emails you ask for, such as confirming your email or resetting your password.

Who can see your journal

Only you. Each journal is protected by database rules that let a signed-in person read and change only their own entries. We do not share your journal with other users.

Service providers

We use a small number of providers to run Edge Journal: Supabase (sign-in and database), Vercel (website hosting), and Google (if you choose Google sign-in). They process data on our behalf under their own terms and privacy policies.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is open. You can export a full backup at any time in Settings, and you can permanently delete your account and all of your journal data from Settings → Delete account.

Children

Edge Journal is not intended for anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
